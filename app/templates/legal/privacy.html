1. Overview & MCP Architecture

This Privacy Policy applies to the Fluxito platform, provided via the hosted web service at fluxito.app and the open-source software project (collectively, "Fluxito", "we", "us", or "our").

Fluxito is an open-source marketing analytics, tag management, and operations layer built around the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Fluxito allows users and their chosen AI assistants (such as Claude Desktop, Claude Cowork, Cursor, Windsurf, ChatGPT, and LM Studio) to securely query analytics data, inspect tag containers, verify tracking plans, and automate reporting across connected third-party platforms.

Unlike traditional marketing SaaS that ingests, aggregates, and monetizes broad customer behavioral datasets, Fluxito operates as an on-demand, authenticated execution proxy. Tools execute only when explicitly triggered by you or your authenticated AI assistant, and data is returned directly into your active session.

2. Zero AI Training Guarantee

Core Principle
We do not use your data to train artificial intelligence models.

Neither Fluxito nor any third party acting on our behalf uses your marketing data, Google API data, SQL queries, tracking plans, conversation transcripts, or confidential business context to train, retrain, fine-tune, align, or evaluate any public, proprietary, or third-party artificial intelligence (AI) or machine learning (ML) models, including Large Language Models (LLMs).

3. Information We Collect

We collect only the information strictly necessary to provide the Fluxito service and execute tool requests:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your email address, full name, and password hash (for email/password accounts) or your Google account ID and basic profile details (when signing in via Google OAuth).
  • Third-Party Platform Credentials: When you connect analytics, advertising, or tag management platforms (such as Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, BigQuery, Meta, TikTok, or Snowflake), we store the OAuth access and refresh tokens, API keys, or service account credentials you authorize.
  • Audit & Telemetry Logs: For security, rate-limiting, and audit compliance, we log tool execution metadata (such as tool name, timestamp, execution duration, requesting user ID, active project, and status). We do not persist raw query payload responses in permanent application logs.
  • Configuration & Workspace Data: Solution Design References (tracking plans), tag audit rules, scheduled report configurations, and project membership mappings you create.

5. How We Use Information

We use the data collected strictly for the following purposes:

  • To provide, operate, maintain, and improve the Fluxito MCP service and web dashboard.
  • To execute MCP tool calls initiated by your connected AI clients (such as Claude, Cursor, Windsurf, or ChatGPT) on your behalf.
  • To verify tracking plans against live website tags and provide tag drift alerts.
  • To generate and deliver scheduled marketing reports and anomaly notifications that you configure.
  • To maintain audit trails so you have full visibility into which tools and platforms your AI assistant accessed.
  • To enforce workspace security, authenticate sessions, and prevent abuse or unauthorized access.

6. Security & Encryption

Security is fundamental to Fluxito's architecture. We implement rigorous technical and operational safeguards:

  • Encryption at Rest: All stored OAuth access tokens, refresh tokens, client secrets, and platform API credentials are encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption keys (cryptographic Fernet).
  • Encryption in Transit: All data transmissions between your browser, AI clients, Fluxito, and external APIs are encrypted using modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Workspace & Project Isolation: Multi-tenant workspaces are strictly isolated at the database schema and query layers. A token issued for Project A can never access or query credentials from Project B.
  • Least Privilege & Human-in-the-loop: Integrations are read-only by default. Where write actions exist (such as publishing GTM tags), Fluxito requires explicit diff confirmation before applying changes.

7. Retention & Revocation

You have continuous, unilateral control over your credentials and connections:

  • Immediate Disconnection: You can disconnect any Google service or third-party platform at any time from Settings → Connections. When disconnected, the associated OAuth tokens and keys are immediately and permanently purged from our database.
  • Revocation via Google: You can revoke Fluxito's OAuth access at any time directly through your Google Account Permissions dashboard.
  • Account Deletion: You can request full deletion of your user account and all workspace data by emailing privacy@fluxito.app. Upon deletion, all projects, audit logs, and credentials are permanently destroyed within 30 days.

8. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal or platform data. We share data only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Your AI Clients: When you connect an MCP client (such as Claude, Cursor, or Windsurf) to your Fluxito endpoint, tool results are transmitted directly to that client session as instructed by your prompt.
  • Infrastructure Service Providers: We utilize trusted cloud hosting, database, and Redis providers (such as AWS, Google Cloud Platform, or DigitalOcean) solely for hosting and running our application infrastructure under strict confidentiality and security agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to do so by law, court order, or governmental authority.

9. Your Privacy Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your location, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: The right to request copies of your personal data held by Fluxito.
  • Right to Rectification: The right to correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): The right to request that we delete your personal data and stored credentials.
  • Right to Data Portability: The right to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format (e.g. tracking plan JSON/CSV exports).
  • Right to Object or Restrict Processing: The right to limit or object to our processing of your personal data.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at privacy@fluxito.app.
