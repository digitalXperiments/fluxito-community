2. Description of Service

Fluxito provides an open-source analytics, tag management, and operations layer accessible through web interfaces and the Model Context Protocol (MCP). Key capabilities include:

  • Exposing standardized MCP tools over SSE and HTTP to authorized AI assistants (such as Claude, Cursor, Windsurf, ChatGPT, and LM Studio);
  • Enabling authenticated, on-demand querying of marketing and analytics platforms (e.g. Google Analytics 4, Google Tag Manager, Google Ads, BigQuery, Meta, TikTok);
  • Generating, validating, and versioning Solution Design References (tracking plans) from live container data;
  • Live tag audits against industry best practice rulebooks; and
  • Automated marketing recaps, dashboard generation, and anomaly detection.
